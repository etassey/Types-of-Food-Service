🏥Hospitals
🧓Long-Term Care
🏫Schools
🔒Correctional
Hospital foodservice must coordinate with clinical staff to deliver therapeutic (modified) diets ordered by physicians and dietitians, often using room-service or tray-line delivery systems.
Long-term care (skilled nursing/assisted living) foodservice focuses on consistent, familiar meals for residents who may stay for months or years, often with texture-modified diets for chewing/swallowing needs.
School foodservice (K-12 or college) often follows federal nutrition guidelines (e.g., National School Lunch Program) and must serve large numbers of students within tight time windows.
Correctional foodservice must balance strict security protocols, cost control, and nutritional adequacy while feeding large populations on fixed schedules.
